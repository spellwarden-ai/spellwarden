# Code of conduct

This applies to the issues, pull requests and discussions of the Spellwarden repositories.

## What we ask

- Be respectful. Disagree with the argument, not with the person.
- Be specific. A report with a version, a sample and what you expected helps everyone who reads it later.
- Assume good faith, and give others room to be wrong and to change their mind.
- Keep other people's private information out of the repositories: no personal data, no customer content, no credentials.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or sexual language, in public or in private messages that follow from a discussion here.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Samples of malicious content that are not made harmless first, or instructions whose purpose is to attack a named person, project or company.

## What the maintainers do

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove a comment, an issue or a pull request that breaks these rules, and may block an account that keeps breaking them. They say why when they do.

## Reporting

Write to `hello@spellwarden.ai` and say what happened and where. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not shared beyond what is needed to act on them.
